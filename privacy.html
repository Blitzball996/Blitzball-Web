Privacy Policy

Blitzball Labs, a brand operated by Beijing Shandianqiu Entertainment Co., Ltd. (北京市闪电球娱乐有限公司) ("we", "us"), builds local-first software. Our applications run on your own hardware and can work fully offline — Blitz DAW and CloseCrab do not require us to collect your projects, audio or code. This Privacy Policy explains the limited personal data we do collect through our website, accounts and licensing system, how we use it, and your rights. It applies to blitzball.lol and our online services, not to data that stays on your device.

★Our approach

We try to collect as little as possible. The desktop apps are local-first; your creative work stays on your machine unless you choose to share it. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not store full payment card numbers.

01What We Collect

  • Account data — if you create an account: your email address, an optional display name, and a hashed password. We never store your password in plain text.
  • Purchase & license data — your purchase records, license serial keys and, for activation, a device fingerprint/identifier used to enforce one-key-one-device binding. Payment is handled by our payment provider; we receive a confirmation and limited transaction metadata, not your full card number.
  • Website analytics — basic, privacy-respecting metrics such as page views, approximate location (country/region/city derived from IP), referrer and device/browser type, collected by our self-hosted analytics. Whether your raw IP address is stored is controlled by a server setting and kept to the minimum needed.
  • Support communications — the contents of emails or messages you send us.

02How We Use It

  • To provide and maintain your account, licenses and the Service.
  • To process purchases and issue/activate license keys (one-key-one-device).
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To understand aggregate website usage and improve our products.
  • To detect, prevent and address fraud, abuse or security issues.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

03Legal Bases

Where applicable law (such as the GDPR) requires it, we rely on: performance of our contract with you (accounts, licenses, purchases); your consent (where asked, e.g. optional analytics); and our legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, product improvement), balanced against your rights.

04Sharing

We share personal data only with:

  • Payment providers (e.g. Airwallex) to process your purchases.
  • Infrastructure and hosting providers that run our servers.
  • Authorities where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data or share it for third-party advertising.

05Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal browser storage. Our website stores your language preference in localStorage (key bb_lang) so your chosen language persists. Account login may use a session cookie or token to keep you signed in. Our self-hosted analytics is designed to be lightweight and does not use third-party advertising cookies.

06Data Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above: account and license data for as long as your account/license is active and as required for our records, tax and legal obligations; analytics data for a limited period in aggregate form. When no longer needed, data is deleted or anonymized.

07Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including password hashing, encrypted connections (HTTPS) and signed license tokens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information and limit what we collect.

08Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, export or restrict processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@blitzball.lol. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

09International Transfers

Our servers and providers may be located in regions different from yours, so your data may be transferred and processed across borders. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10Children

The Service is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice where appropriate. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.

12Contact

Questions about your privacy or this Policy? Contact us at privacy@blitzball.lol.
