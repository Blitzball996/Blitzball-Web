▌ How it works

Three minutes from desk to pocket.

CloseCrab Mobile is a tiny local service that bridges your phone to the CloseCrab already running on your PC.

1Activate CloseCrab on your PC. The desktop app stores your license — Mobile Remote reuses it, so there's no second login on your phone.
2Run closecrab-web. It verifies this machine's license, then prints a token-protected URL and a Cloudflare tunnel link.
3Open the link on your phone. Tap "New Session" and you're driving CloseCrab — over your private mesh network or the tunnel.
▌ Remote access

Three ways to reach home.

Pick whichever fits your network. All keep traffic private — nothing routes through our servers.

01Recommended

Tailscale

Install on both PC and phone, sign in with the same account. Your devices get a private mesh IP — no port forwarding.

http://<pc-tailscale-ip>:3000
02

ZeroTier

Friendlier inside China. Join both devices to the same network, then reach the PC on its ZeroTier address.

http://<pc-zerotier-ip>:3000
03

Cloudflare Tunnel

Built in. CloseCrab opens a cloudflared tunnel on launch and prints a public URL — open it on any device, no VPN.

https://<random>.trycloudflare.com

🔒 Built to fail closed

  • License-bound. Mobile Remote only starts on a machine with an active CloseCrab license and remote enabled — verified on every launch.
  • Token-gated. Every session needs a token. No token, no shell — there is no anonymous mode by default.
  • Owner kill-switch. Turn remote access off for any device from your account page; the next launch is refused.
  • Local-first. The bridge runs on your PC. Code, keys, and model never touch a third-party server.
